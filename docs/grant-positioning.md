# Grant Positioning

ColorChain should be presented as an independent, protocol-agnostic, mobile-first P2P network for resilient public-interest information sharing.

## Core positioning

- Independent decentralized infrastructure.
- Mobile-first peer-to-peer design.
- Verifiable block-based persistence.
- Transparent AI-assisted trust signals.
- Open protocol direction with public-interest value.

## What to emphasize

- ColorChain reduces dependency on centralized publishing platforms.
- ColorChain supports resilient information sharing under unstable conditions.
- ColorChain is designed for direct node-to-node operation across mobile and desktop environments.
- ColorChain combines transport, persistence, and advisory trust metadata in one independent network stack.
- ColorChain is suitable for prototype grants, public-interest technology grants, resilience infrastructure programs, and open protocol research support.

## What to avoid

- Do not present ColorChain as dependent on any external blockchain ecosystem.
- Do not frame it as a thin layer on top of another network.
- Do not claim the AI layer makes final truth decisions.
- Do not overstate production readiness.

## Suggested one-line description

ColorChain is an independent mobile-first peer-to-peer infrastructure for resilient, verifiable, and censorship-resistant public-interest information sharing.
